Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 27, 2025

Data Storage

VaultPad is local-first. Your notes and files stay in your browser's IndexedDB. We do not have a database on our servers. Since we never receive your data, we cannot read it.

Log Files

Standard technical logs are used for security and performance analysis. These logs do not contain your private workspace content or secret pad names.

Google Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads based upon their visit to VaultPad.

View Google Ad Policy →

Advertising

Advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons on our site. These third-party ad servers use technology to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and personalize the content you see.

GDPR & CCPA

We respect your data rights. Because your data is local to your device, you have 100% control over its access and deletion. Clearing your browser cache permanently removes all local data from this device.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our local-first security model, please contact our technical team.
