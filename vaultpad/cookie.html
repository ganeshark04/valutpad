Cookie Policy

Last Updated: December 27, 2025

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of text sent by your web browser by a website you visit. They allow the site or a third-party to recognize you and make your next visit easier.

How we use them

VaultPad uses cookies for technical reasons and advertising. VaultPad does not use cookies to store your secret notes; those are kept in secure local browser storage.

Essential Local Storage

VaultPad uses LocalStorage and IndexedDB to function. These allow you to save your notes and files locally on your device without a cloud account.

Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to deliver contextual ads. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites.

View Google Ad Policy →

Your Choices

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies via your browser settings. If you reject cookies, access to some functionality may be restricted.
